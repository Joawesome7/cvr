Who we are

A family business
rooted in Cavite

Carbo-Valencia Realty Inc. started 2012 when Marietta Carbo-Valencia passed the board of Real Estate Brokers Examination. Through effort and perseverance the company became a leading realty firm in Cavite — and following her footsteps, her sons became licensed Real Estate Brokers and Appraisers.

Carbo-Valencia Realty Inc. is registered with the Securities and Exchange Commission, SSS, PAG-IBIG, PhilHealth, and the Bureau of Internal Revenue. We operate with full transparency and faith.
